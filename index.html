Perspective on AI and Developer Education

I believe in the Tony Stark model: developers must be deeply skilled so that AI tools genuinely augment their capabilities rather than create a false sense of competence. Tony Stark is not less important because of Jarvis — he is amplified. He makes the decisions, sometimes overriding Jarvis's recommendations. That requires deep expertise.

As AI capabilities advance, developer education must focus more on fundamentals, critical thinking, and the ability to evaluate AI-generated output — not less. The developers who will thrive are those who can direct AI tools with genuine understanding, not those who delegate understanding to them.
